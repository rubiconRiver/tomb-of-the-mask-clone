# Rhythm Tomb - Game Proposal

## Concept
Grid-based swipe puzzle game with optional rhythm mechanics. Players navigate mazes Tomb of the Mask style, but can score bonus points by moving on the beat.

**Unique selling point**: Free movement with optional rhythm - players choose their playstyle.

## MVP Requirements
**Core principle: Ship a polished, professional game we're proud to show**

- 10-20 hand-crafted levels
- Smooth, satisfying movement and animations  
- Cohesive visual style (no placeholder graphics)
- Single polished music theme
- Slick UI with proper transitions
- iOS release ready

## Post-Launch Strategy
**Ship fast, iterate based on player response**

### Content Pipeline
- Weekly level packs
- New music themes (trap, synthwave, lo-fi)
- Character skins
- Special mechanics (platforms, switches)

### Why This Works
- Low barrier to entry (simple swipe controls)
- High skill ceiling (rhythm mastery)
- Easy to create new content
- Can pivot based on metrics

## Next Steps
1. Polish visuals to professional standard

3. Test with small group
4. Ship to App Store
5. Monitor metrics and iterate
