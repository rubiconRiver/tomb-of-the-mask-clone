# 🎛️ Synth Editor - User Guide

## Quick Start
**Press 'A' during gameplay** to open the Synth Editor and customize your game's sounds in real-time!

## Overview
The Synth Editor is a streamlined sound design tool that lets you shape all 6 game sounds with intuitive controls. No complex synthesis knowledge required - just adjust sliders and hear the results instantly.

## How to Access
- **In Game**: Press the **'A' key** while playing
- **Exit**: Press **ESC** or click the **✕** button to close

## Interface Layout

### 🎵 Sound Selection
**Dropdown Menu** at the top lets you choose which sound to edit:
- **Kick Drum** - Deep bass hits on beats 1 & 3
- **Snare Drum** - Crisp snare on beats 2 & 4  
- **Hi-Hat** - Metallic hi-hat on 8th notes
- **Coin Chime** - Pleasant chimes when collecting coins
- **Wall Hit Tom** - Punchy tom sequence when hitting walls
- **Guitar Chord** - Ambient chord during movement

### 🎚️ Parameter Controls
**Easy-to-use sliders** for each sound with **real-time value display**:

#### Kick Drum Parameters:
- **Pitch** (40-100 Hz) - How deep/high the kick sounds
- **Punch** (0.1-2.0) - Impact and attack strength
- **Decay** (0.05-0.3s) - How long the kick lasts
- **Click** (0.0-0.5) - Sharp attack for punch

#### Snare Drum Parameters:
- **Tone** (100-400 Hz) - Fundamental frequency
- **Noise** (0.0-1.0) - Balance between tone and noise
- **Snap** (0.05-0.2s) - Duration of the hit
- **Crisp** (20-60) - How sharp the attack is

#### Hi-Hat Parameters:
- **Brightness** (6k-15k Hz) - High-frequency content
- **Sharpness** (50-150) - How crisp the decay is
- **Length** (0.02-0.1s) - Duration of the hit
- **Metallic** (0.0-1.0) - Amount of metallic shimmer

#### Coin Chime Parameters:
- **Pitch** (800-2000 Hz) - Base frequency
- **Shimmer** (0.0-1.0) - Harmonic richness
- **Sustain** (0.1-0.4s) - How long it rings
- **Harmony** (0-2) - Chord type (Major/Sus2/Minor)

#### Wall Hit Tom Parameters:
- **Low Tom** (80-150 Hz) - Frequency of lowest tom
- **Mid Tom** (120-200 Hz) - Frequency of middle tom
- **High Tom** (150-250 Hz) - Frequency of highest tom
- **Power** (0.3-0.8s) - Total duration of the fill

#### Guitar Chord Parameters:
- **Warmth** (200-600 Hz) - Base frequency character
- **Richness** (0.0-1.0) - Harmonic content
- **Vibrato** (0.0-0.02) - Pitch modulation amount
- **Ambient** (0.1-0.5) - Overall volume level

### 📊 Waveform Preview
**Real-time visualization** shows the shape of your sound as you adjust parameters. Each sound type has its own color:
- 🔴 Kick Drum - Red
- 🟡 Snare Drum - Yellow  
- 🔵 Hi-Hat - Cyan
- 🟠 Coin Chime - Gold
- 🟣 Wall Hit Tom - Magenta
- 🟢 Guitar Chord - Green

### ▶️ Preview & Apply
- **Preview Button** - Click to hear your current sound settings
- **Real-time Updates** - Waveform updates instantly as you move sliders
- **Non-destructive** - Original sounds are preserved until you're satisfied

## Professional Sound Design Tips

### 🥁 **Drum Sounds (Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat)**
- **Lower pitch** = deeper, more powerful
- **Higher decay/sharpness** = tighter, more controlled
- **Add click/crisp** for punch and clarity in the mix

### 🎵 **Melodic Sounds (Coin Chime)**
- **Higher pitch** = brighter, more cheerful
- **More shimmer** = richer, more complex harmonics
- **Longer sustain** = more musical, less percussive

### 🎸 **Ambient Sounds (Guitar Chord)**
- **Lower warmth** = darker, more mysterious
- **Higher richness** = fuller, more lush
- **Subtle vibrato** = more organic, less static

### 💪 **Impact Sounds (Wall Hit Tom)**
- **Descending tom frequencies** create satisfying "du-du-DUH" effect
- **Longer power** = more dramatic impact
- **Tune toms to musical intervals** for harmony

## Workflow Tips

1. **Start with defaults** - All parameters begin at musically useful values
2. **Make small adjustments** - Big changes can sound harsh
3. **Preview frequently** - Hear changes immediately
4. **Compare with originals** - A/B test your modifications
5. **Focus on one sound** - Perfect each individually
6. **Consider the mix** - How sounds work together in gameplay
